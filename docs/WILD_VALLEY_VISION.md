# Panda & Ape — Wild Valley

A welcoming camp grows into a shared woodland home. Explore → gather → prepare
soil → plant → water → harvest → sell → build and improve. Combat and the shrine
remain optional adventures alongside the garden, not prerequisites to farming.

The first farm sits south of Rowan. Shared supplies let either hero complete the
whole loop alone; Panda can water an adjacent plot, Ape saves one wood when
building. Short, visible game days make first harvests accessible. Unwatered
crops wait indefinitely. No hunger, deadlines, plant death or offline penalties.

Four crops offer distinct silhouettes, growth times and margins. Rowan supplies
seeds and buys produce; wood, stone, fibre and ore come from renewable local
resource caches. Clear placement feedback and a first crafted workbench turn
harvest profits and gathered supplies into a visible home improvement.

Future priorities: richer processing at workbenches; durable settlement selection
and management; rain, irrigation and seasonal discovery. Existing original art
and hero identities guide every addition. No referenced game's art/layout is used.
